## What did we learn from being grilled?

The most challenging question was whether our MVP genuinely simulates a 14-day-ahead recommendation or simply uses information from a final historical dataset. It exposed the importance of preventing future-information leakage and distinguishing expected cancellations from future bookings. We now understand that the Build Lead must coordinate with the Model Lead, validate the model interface and ensure that the nightly workflow uses only appropriate information and frozen settings.
